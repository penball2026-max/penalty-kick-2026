Privacy Policy

App: Penalty Kick 2026
Last updated: April 1, 2026

1. Overview

Penalty Kick 2026 ("the app") is a free soccer penalty-kick game for Android. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

2. Data We Collect

The app itself does not collect or store any personal information. We do not require account registration, and we do not transmit any gameplay data to our servers.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location
  • Device model, OS version, and language
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Please review Google's privacy policy for details:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized advertising via your device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

4. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob is configured to serve ads appropriate for general audiences.

5. Data Retention

We do not store any user data. Any data collected by AdMob is governed by Google's own retention policies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at the same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
